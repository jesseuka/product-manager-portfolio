My Product Thinking Process

I believe good products start with a clear understanding of the problem. My approach is to understand the user, define the problem, explore possible solutions, prioritize what matters and work toward measurable outcomes.


Understand → Define → Research → Ideate → Prioritize → Define Requirements → Design → Test → Measure
